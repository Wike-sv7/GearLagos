About GEAR LAGOS

GEAR LAGOS was founded in 2018 with a simple mission: to give Lagosians and visitors access to reliable, affordable, and well-maintained vehicles without the stress that usually comes with car hire in Nigeria.

What started as a small fleet of three cars in Ikeja has grown into one of Lagos' most trusted vehicle rental services, covering the Island, Mainland, Lekki, Ajah, Surulere, and beyond. We take pride in our transparent pricing, verified drivers, and fully insured vehicles.

Whether you need a car for an hour, a day, or a month — GEAR LAGOS has you covered.
